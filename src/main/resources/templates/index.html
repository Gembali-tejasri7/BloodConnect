About BloodConnect

BloodConnect is an online blood donor coordination platform designed to connect voluntary blood donors with recipients. It helps people find potential donors based on blood group and location and coordinate blood donation requests.

How It Works

1. Register

Create an account and provide your basic details.

2. Find Donors

Search for potential donors by blood group and location.

3. Send Request

Send a blood donation coordination request.

4. Save Lives

Donors can respond to requests and coordinate further.
